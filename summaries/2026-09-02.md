# llama.cpp daily summary -- 2026-09-02

A busy day dominated by Metal/Apple Silicon improvements (tunings for M2 Pro/Max, A18 Pro, and Metal 4.0 enablement on M5+/A19+), a notable CUDA MoE kernel fusion for performance, and a round of Qwen4 experimental model fixes.
