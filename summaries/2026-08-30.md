## OpenCL

- **better matmul path on Adreno X2E and A7X** -- enables the xmem F16xF32 GEMM by default on Adreno X2E (worth ~25% prefill speedup on gpt-oss-20b) and bypasses the tiled F32 GEMM on the A7X where the compiler spills badly (~9% prefill gain on gemma-3n-E4B). Override with env vars `GGML_OPENCL_A7X_F32_LM_BYPASS=0`. PR #27640 `c841aeeb`

## SYCL

- **--fit respects --fit-target better** -- improves VRAM-fitting algorithm to account for actual peak VRAM at a given context size, fixing OOM cases and reducing overly-conservative reservation. Validated on Arc B70 with 262144-token context. PR #27629 `cc83d7b4`

## Vulkan

- **mul_mat_id: pad K instead of N** -- fixes a correctness issue where the wrong dimension was padded for expert-gating matrix multiply; N-padding is needed for mul_mat but K-padding is what mul_mat_id actually checks. PR #27925 `77f132cb`
- **consolidate fastdiv helpers** -- deduplicates fastdiv functions scattered across six shader files into utils.glsl; renames the small-divisor variant for clarity. Pure refactor, no behaviour change. PR #27526 `c9ca51c1`
